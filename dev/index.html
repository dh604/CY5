Welcome!

This website accompanies the paper Experiments with Membranes, Maps and Sheaves by Yannik Schuler and Daniel Holmes [HS26]. It consists of two parts:

  • Computations in Gromov-Witten theory using GW_CY5.jl, a Julia package to compute the membrane indices $\Omega_\beta$ of toric CY5s and check the conjectural formulas in [HS26]. It builds on GKMtools.jl [HM25a, HM25b] to compute equivariant Gromov-Witten invariants of the relevant spaces.
  • Computations in Pandharipande-Thomas theory using PT_CY5.sage, leading to the conjectural formulas in [HS26]. This builds on [Liu25].
