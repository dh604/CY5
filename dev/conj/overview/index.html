Overview of GW evidence

In this section, we explain how our GW calculations cited as Evidence in [HS26] can be reproduced.

EvidenceSpaceMain function(s)
Evidence 2.8$X\times\mathbb{C}^2$ where $X$ is a 3d strip geometrygkm_5d_strip
Evidence 2.13$X\times\mathbb{C}^2$ where $X$ is the closed vertexgkm_5d_closed_vertex
Evidence 3.5$X_{N,m}\times\mathbb{C}^2$ (GW-gauge correspondence)gkm_5d_gauge
Evidence 4.2$\text{Tot}(\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^2}(-1)^{\oplus 3})$gkm_5d_P2_111
Evidence 4.8$\text{Tot}(\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^3}(-a)\oplus\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^3}(a-4))$gkm_5d_P3_04/_13/_22
Evidence 5.5$\mathbb{C}\times\left(\text{Tot}\,\mathcal{O}_S(-D_1)\oplus \mathcal{O}_S(-D_1)\right)$CY5_from_CY4

In the last row, we treat the cases $S=\mathbb{P}^2$ with $\mathcal{O}(-1)\oplus\mathcal{O}(-2)$ and $S=\mathbb{P}^1\times \mathbb{P}^1$ with $\mathcal{O}(-1,-1)\oplus\mathcal{O}(-1,-1)$ and $\mathcal{O}(-2, 0)\oplus\mathcal{O}(0, -2)$.

Note

Note that we executed most of the code listed in this section on a compute cluster provided by the Mathematics Department of ETH Zürich with the following metrics.

  • Operating System: Fedora
  • CPU: 2x Intel Xeon Gold 6254 (18 Cores) 3.1 GHz
  • RAM: 512 GB

Therefore, it may be hard to reproduce some of our examples on an ordinary PC.
